What it is

A remote, not a cloud.

Hermes is a self‑hosted AI agent gateway. It runs profiles: independent agents, each with its own model, tools, memory and personality, on a machine you own. Vory connects to that gateway and turns every profile into a bot: a small living character with a body, eyes and a colour, drawn in Liquid Glass. On your iPhone, and now on your Mac.

Your machine

Your agents run where you put them. Vory talks to your gateway directly, over your LAN, Tailscale or a Cloudflare Tunnel. No port‑forwarding, no middleman.

Your call

When a bot wants to run a shell command or change a file, it waits for your yes. From the chat, from a notification, from the Lock Screen.

Your cast

Every agent is a character, so a house of bots is a cast, not a list of sessions. You always know who is talking and what they are doing.

New · Vory for Mac

The same Vory, in a window.

A real Mac app, built for a window, a keyboard and the menu bar. The same bots, the same chats, the same places as the phone, in the same TestFlight beta.

One chat, open on both. Send from the Mac and it lands on the iPhone as you watch.
A window and a keyboard

A rail of pages on the left, the chat list beside the open chat, the thread in a reading column. Return sends, Shift‑Return adds a line, drop files or paste images into the composer. ⌘N, ⌘F, ⌘. and the rest.

The menu bar

Running turns and waiting approvals live in the menu bar with Approve and Deny. The Dock icon carries a badge for what needs you. Close the window and the menu bar item stays; Open Vory in it, or the Dock icon, brings the window back.

Widgets and files

Widgets for the desktop and Notification Center. Drop files on the Files page to upload, drag one out to the Finder. Notifications come through the same Companion as the phone.

The 1.3 film: both devices on one chat, an approval answered on one and cleared on the other, iCloud restore, projects, the status line, and each device’s own surface.

Two devices, one app

Pick up on either one.

Nothing to set up between them beyond your own iCloud. Vory still hosts nothing and has no account.

Your setup follows you

Settings, bot looks, your name on Home and your saved gateways sync through your own iCloud. A new device offers Restore from iCloud on its first screen, and one tap brings it all back; a gateway that signs in through a browser or a password asks you to sign in once on the new device.

A yes from either one

An approval card reaches both. Answer it on the Mac or from your pocket and it clears everywhere. The reply keeps streaming on both screens.

Projects, side by side

Group chats by project on both devices, and move a chat to another project from its menu. Handoff carries a chat open on one device to the other.

How it works

The loop.

Connect once. Then every turn goes the same way: you ask, a bot thinks, uses its tools, asks you before anything risky, and replies. Its face tells you what it is doing.

1
Connect

Point Vory at your gateway: local network, Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnel, or any URL. Sign in with its token. A guided tour introduces the bots.

2
Meet your bots

Every profile on the gateway appears as a bot. Design it in the Creator Studio: 8 body shapes, 8 eye styles, any colour, flat or Liquid Glass.

3
Talk

Messages‑style chats. Bubbles with tails, typing indicators, streamed replies with live token counts, reasoning you can unfold, tool cards, attachments, “/” commands, steering mid‑reply, dictation.

4
Approve

When a bot needs a yes, an approval card appears in the chat, in a notification, in the Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and in the Mac’s menu bar. Approve and Deny are right there.

5
Get told

The Vory Companion, a small plugin installed on the gateway from the app, sends replies as notifications from the bot on iPhone and Mac, keeps the Live Activity on its phase, and delivers approval requests instantly. Reply from the notification.

6
Group chats

Put two to six bots in one room and let them talk to you and to each other. Each reply carries its bot’s face.

7
Run the gateway

Settings for every profile: model, config, API keys, tools, skills, MCP servers, approvals policy, scheduled tasks, sessions, channels, system health, logs, restarts and updates.

8
Summaries BETA

Apple Intelligence, on‑device, gives every chat a title and a two‑line summary in the list, and a working bot shows the line it is working toward. Nothing leaves the device.

9
Home

A dashboard with your month in numbers, your bots, the chats to pick back up and what changed while you were away. Drag the cards into your order on the phone or the Mac.

Idle bots barely move: a blink every few seconds, a glance to one side, a rare slow blink. Their eyes follow your scroll.

Idle life

Blinks every ~4 s, every third a double blink. A glance every ~7 s. A rare slow blink. Eyes follow where you scroll. Every bot has its own timing, so a page of them never blinks in unison.

Working

Every 3 s, one small routine: the signature coin‑turn, a glance‑turn, a head tilt, a nod, a lean, a scan, a squint‑and‑settle, a light travelling the glass rim.

States you can read at a glance

Thinking becomes a heavier pebble. Using a tool grows a small stem. Waiting for your approval becomes a bold “!” and holds. An error drops its eyes and the light dies. Reconnecting sweeps its eyes like a metronome. Finished: one full turn.

“If it looks like the bots are dancing, cut it. If it looks like one just had a thought, keep it.”

The one rule of Vory motion. Nothing scales, bounces, hops or pops.
