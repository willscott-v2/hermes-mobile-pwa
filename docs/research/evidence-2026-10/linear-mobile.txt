Built for “away from keyboard” activities.
Linear Mobile is purpose-designed for on-demand workflows that complement the desktop experience. A powerful sidekick, always available in your pocket.

Ultraportable. A from-the-ground-up redesigned user interface makes Linear Mobile both light in weight and strong in performance.

Fully native. Linear Mobile is built with native Swift and Kotlin code to guarantee a fast and fluid user experience.

Stay on top of your most important streams of work

The inbox keeps you informed about high-priority tasks and projects that need your attention. Tap to take action, swipe to delete, snooze to deal with it later.

React to what matters most…

Discuss and update issue details on the fly to unblock your team and keep work progressing.

…snooze the rest for later

Temporarily remove notifications from your inbox to focus on what matters in the present moment.

Move product work forward. No matter where you are.

Write whenever inspiration strikes. With its ergonomic, palm-perfect design, Linear Mobile enables you to write issues, comments, and updates from anywhere.

Create issues in seconds

The quick-access issue composer is designed with an obsessive focus on speed. So you can jot down ideas as they come.

From screenshot to triage in a few simple taps

Quickly share screenshots or photos with Linear to initiate new issues or bug reports.

Read and write project updates

Keep everyone informed about the health and progress of your projects.

PRDs-to-go

Review and react to product specs and other documents in a compact, mobile-optimized reading experience.

Available 24/7. Or just 9-5.

Receive push notifications about important updates in realtime (but not all the time).

Realtime notifications. Stay up-to-date on important work streams and instantly take action wherever and whenever necessary.

Notification schedule. Configure your own notification schedule to only stay in touch with your work during certain time periods.
